## S5 · About & Support

### What this is

Shame: The Game is a reflection tool built on cards. Each card uses an image (a cage, a cloak, a labyrinth) to name a kind of shame, a moment that can set it off, or a practice that can help. Choosing cards and reading about them is a way to look at your own experience from a little distance.

### What this isn't

Shame: The Game is not therapy, counseling, or medical or mental health care. It doesn't diagnose anything, and it isn't a substitute for a licensed professional. If you're looking for that kind of support, a doctor or a licensed mental health professional is the right place to start.

### Who made this, and why

Shame: The Game was created by Paul J. Chamberlain, who developed this tool from his own story of changing his relationship with shame. He wrote every card and chose the research the readings draw on.

### How readings are made

When you tap "Read my cards," the names of your four cards are sent to an AI system (Claude, made by Anthropic), which writes a short reading. Nothing else is sent: no name, no email, no account.

Readings follow a few rules:
- They may mention ideas from researchers and writers on shame, compassion and storytelling, but only from a short list we've checked, and only as ideas, never as quotes.
- They don't give statistics or claim that research proves anything.
- They don't tell you what you feel or what happened to you. Only you know that.

AI can still get things wrong. If a reading feels off, trust yourself over the reading, and please tell us through the feedback link below.

### Your privacy

- We don't save your readings or the cards you choose, and we don't keep records of them.
- Your reading exists only on your screen. When you close the page, it's gone. To keep one, use Share.
- There are no ads, no trackers, and no accounts.
- The app remembers one thing on your own device: that you've already seen the welcome screen.

### Talk to someone

If you're struggling, or something here brought up more than you expected, you don't have to sit with it alone.

**988 Suicide & Crisis Lifeline**
Call, text or chat **988**, anytime. It's free, confidential, and caring support for anything that's weighing on you: stress, relationships, substance use, loneliness, or thoughts of suicide. You don't have to be in crisis to reach out.
- Chat: **988lifeline.org**
- Veterans: call 988 and press 1, or text **838255**
- En español: call 988 and press 2, or text **AYUDA** to 988
- Deaf or hard of hearing: use your preferred relay service, or dial **711** then 988

**If you or someone else is in immediate danger, call 911.**

A trusted friend, a doctor, or a licensed mental health professional can also help.

## Notes for Paul

- **Why 988 is described this way:** the 988 Lifeline's own messaging guidance recommends presenting it as free, caring, confidential help for anyone struggling, not only for emergencies, and avoiding comparisons such as "988 is not 911." Most players who feel stirred up won't be in crisis, so this framing fits the game.
- **The LGBTQ+ option ("press 3") is left out on purpose.** It was ended in July 2025 and reported restored on Sept 30, 2026, but as of Oct 1 the 988 site had no instructions for it, and it was built for young people while this app is 18+. Check the 988 site before launch and add it if it's clearly live and open to adults.
- **The 18+ box** is a self-confirmation, not age verification. That's normal for a field test; revisit before public launch.
- **"Who made this"** is a branding and liability choice: you personally, or Cerebral Itch Labs LLC. Counsel can advise which.
- **Words this copy avoids:** heal, healing, treatment, cure, diagnosis (except to say the app doesn't diagnose), and any professional title.

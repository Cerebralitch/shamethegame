Welcome to Shame: The Game

A card game for looking at shame through image and story. Choose four cards, and receive a short reading about how they might speak to your life.

Before you begin

  • This is self-reflection, not therapy. It isn't counseling or mental health care, and it doesn't replace them.
  • Made by a person, written with AI. Shame: The Game was created by Paul J. Chamberlain, who developed this tool from his own story of changing the relationship with his own shame. Your reading is generated by AI, guided by his card meanings and a checked set of research on shame. It can still miss the mark, so take what's useful and leave the rest.
  • Nothing is saved. We don't keep your readings or the cards you choose.
  • Support is always one tap away. If anything stirs up more than you expected, you'll find people to talk to under About.

About & Support

What this is

Shame: The Game is a reflection tool built on cards. Each card uses an image (a cage, a cloak, a labyrinth) to name a kind of shame, a moment that can set it off, or a practice that can help.

What this isn't

Shame: The Game is not therapy, counseling, or medical or mental health care. It doesn't diagnose anything, and it isn't a substitute for a licensed professional.

Your privacy

We don't save your readings or the cards you choose. Your reading exists only on your screen. There are no ads, no trackers and no accounts.

Talk to someone

988 Suicide & Crisis Lifeline. Call, text or chat 988, anytime. It's free, confidential, and caring support for anything that's weighing on you. You don't have to be in crisis to reach out. Chat at 988lifeline.org.

Veterans: call 988 and press 1, or text 838255. En español: call 988 and press 2, or text AYUDA to 988. Deaf or hard of hearing: use your relay service, or dial 711 then 988.

If you or someone else is in immediate danger, call 911.
